Services for every coat

Every appointment starts with a coat check and a chat — then a groom tailored to your pet's breed, temperament and lifestyle. Call 0161 866 8000 for pricing and advice.

Most popular

Full Groom

The complete pamper package. We bathe, dry, brush and style your pet from nose to tail, finishing with those little details that make all the difference.

  • Warm bath with quality, pet-safe shampoo
  • Fluff-dry and thorough brush-out
  • Full cut or breed-aware trim
  • Nail clip and ear tidy included

Bath & Brush

The perfect in-between service. After muddy walks along the Bridgewater or a dusty week at the park, we get your dog clean, soft and smelling lovely — no full cut needed.

  • Warm bath and gentle rinse
  • Full brush-out to lift tangles
  • Blow-dry and finishing spray
  • Optional nail trim on request

Puppy Intro

A short, happy first visit designed to make the salon a fun place. We introduce your puppy to the sounds, smells and gentle handling of grooming — with treats, play and zero pressure.

  • Calm, positive first experience
  • Gentle brush and light tidy
  • Nail familiarity and handling practice
  • Advice for home coat care

De-shedding Treatment

For double-coated breeds blowing their coat — huskies, retrievers, shepherds and friends. A deep treatment that lifts loose undercoat safely, so your sofa (and your hoover) gets a break.

  • Deep-cleansing bath and coat loosening
  • High-velocity undercoat removal
  • De-matting where needed, handled kindly
  • Lighter, healthier-feeling coat
