Our story

A decade of wagging tails on Barton Road

Aliwags opened its doors in 2015 with a simple idea: a grooming salon where pets are treated like family and owners are treated like neighbours. Eleven years on, we're still independent, still local, and still greeted by name when we pop to the shops.

Our multi-staff team means your pet is never rushed and there's always a familiar face on hand. We take the time to get to know nervous dogs, wriggly puppies and senior pets who need a gentler touch.

  • Gentle, patient handlingWe go at your pet's pace — breaks, cuddles and encouragement included.
  • A real local salonBased at 424 Barton Road, Stretford — part of the Trafford community.
  • Consistent, careful finishesBreed-aware trims and tidy-ups, checked twice before your pet goes home.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?
Just call us on 0161 866 8000. We like to have a proper chat about your pet first so we book the right amount of time and the right groomer for their temperament.
What are your opening hours?
We're open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, 9:30am–6pm. We're closed on Wednesdays, Saturdays and Sundays so the team can rest — and so can the salon cats.
Do you groom nervous or first-time dogs?
Absolutely — it's one of the things we're known for. Our puppy intro visits are short, gentle sessions, and for nervous adults we go at their pace with breaks and plenty of reassurance. Tell us about their worries when you book.
Do you groom cats?
We're primarily a dog grooming salon, but we're happy to discuss confident cats for tidy-ups and de-matting. Give us a ring and we'll be honest about whether we're the right fit for your cat.
How long will my pet be with you?
It depends on the coat and the service — a tidy-up can be under an hour, while a full groom on a large, thick-coated dog takes longer. We'll give you a realistic time when you book and call you the moment they're ready.
